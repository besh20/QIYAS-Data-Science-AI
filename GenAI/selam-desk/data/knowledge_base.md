## Registration Requirements
To register, new students need their national ID or kebele ID, two passport-size
photos, their grade 12 transcript or previous semester transcript, and proof of
fee payment. Returning students need their student ID and proof of fee payment.

## Registration Fee
The registration fee is 500 ETB for undergraduate students and 800 ETB for
graduate students. Payment can be made at the university's finance office or
via bank transfer, with the receipt brought to the registration office.

## Registration Dates
Registration for this semester opens on the first Monday of the month and
closes after two weeks. Late registration is allowed for one additional week
with a 100 ETB late fee, after which students must petition the registrar.

## Where to Register
Registration takes place at the Registrar's Office, Building A, Room 105,
open Monday to Friday from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. There is currently no online
registration option; all registration is done in person.

## Common Problems
If a student's ID card is lost, they should report to the Registrar's Office
with a police report or affidavit to get a replacement, which takes about
three working days. If documents are incomplete, students may submit
provisionally and complete the file within one week.

## Contact / Escalation
For issues not covered here -- complaints, rejected documents, or anything
requiring a decision -- students should be connected to a Registrar's Office
staff member directly rather than given an automated answer.
